Cookies and Privacy

How Cookies Affect Privacy

Cookies can make browsing easier by remembering your preferences. However, some cookies can also help companies track browsing activity. This information may be used to learn about your interests and show you personalized ads.

First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies belong to the website you are visiting. They often help with features such as signing in or remembering settings. Third-party cookies belong to a different website, such as an advertising service that has content on the page. They can be used to track activity across different websites.

Ways to Manage Your Privacy

  • Review the cookie choices a website offers before accepting.
  • Reject optional advertising cookies when that choice is available.
  • Use your browser settings to limit third-party cookies.
  • Delete saved cookies when needed, but remember this may sign you out.
  • Read privacy notices to understand how a website uses your information.

Cookies are only one way websites can track activity. Managing them gives you more control, but it does not stop every type of online tracking.
