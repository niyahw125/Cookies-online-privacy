Key Concepts

These terms help explain how cookies and online privacy work.

Cookies
Small pieces of data saved in your browser that help websites remember information between visits or page requests.
First-party cookies
Cookies associated with the website you are visiting. They can help remember your preferences or sign-in session.
Third-party cookies
Cookies associated with a different website whose content appears on the page. Some are used to track browsing across websites.
Online tracking
Collecting information about what people do online, such as which pages they visit or which links they click.
Privacy settings
Controls that let you manage some of the information your browser or a website collects and shares.
Cookie consent
Giving a website permission to use certain cookies, often through choices shown in a cookie banner.
