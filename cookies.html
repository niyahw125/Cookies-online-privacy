How Cookies Work

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website stores in your browser. They help websites remember things, such as your language preference or whether you are signed in.

Why Do Websites Use Them?

Without cookies, a website may not remember you as you move from one page to another. For example, an online store can use a cookie to connect your browser to your shopping cart, so your items stay there while you keep shopping.

How Does It Work?

  1. You visit a website.
  2. The website asks your browser to save a cookie.
  3. If allowed, your browser stores it.
  4. On later requests to that website, your browser can send the cookie back so the site remembers information.
